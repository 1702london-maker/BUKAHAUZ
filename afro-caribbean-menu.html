Tasting Menu

An evocative journey through the Atlantic, blending ancestral West African foundations with vibrant Caribbean expression.

The Essence of Fusion

At Bukahauz, our Afro-Caribbean menu is a living archive of flavor. We bridge the distance between the shores of Lagos and the hills of St. Elizabeth, using the 48-hour marination techniques of our elders and the precision of modern gastronomy.

"Every spice tells a story of migration, resilience, and unyielding joy." — Chef de Cuisine
