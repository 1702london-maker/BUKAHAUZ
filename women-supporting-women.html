Frequently Asked Questions

Who is this circle for? expand_more

The initiative is designed for women leaders, founders, and creatives who are committed to both their own growth and the elevation of others. We welcome diverse backgrounds across technology, art, finance, and social enterprise.

Are dinners open to non-members? expand_more

Occasionally we host open evenings, but the majority of our curated dinners are reserved exclusively for members to maintain the intimacy and depth of the connection.

How are mentorship pairings decided? expand_more

We use a combination of shared goals, complementary skills, and personality alignment to ensure mentorship pairs are mutually beneficial and highly effective.
