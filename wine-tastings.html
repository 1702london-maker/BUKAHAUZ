The Sommeliers

"Wine is a conversation, not a lecture."

Led by our Master Sommelier, the Bukahauz approach strips away the pretension of traditional tastings. We focus on the 'why' behind the wine—the history of the soil, the intent of the winemaker, and the way the vintage interacts with the soul of our menu.

Do you provide the glassware?

expand_more

Yes, for all private home tastings, we provide professional Riedel crystal glassware, decanters, and tasting mats.

expand_more

Absolutely. We can tailor flights to specific regions, vintages, or price points based on your preference.

How long do the sessions last?

expand_more

Standard sessions are 90 to 120 minutes, allowing for deep discussion and slow enjoyment of the pairings.
