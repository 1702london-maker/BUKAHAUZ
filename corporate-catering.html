Common Inquiries

What is the minimum lead time for a corporate booking? expand_more
For standard business lunches, we recommend 48 hours notice. For large-scale events or bespoke launch parties, we typically require 2-4 weeks of lead time to ensure every detail is perfect.
Do you accommodate specific dietary restrictions? expand_more
Absolutely. We specialize in inclusive catering. Our chefs are experts at crafting allergen-friendly, vegan, and religious-compliant menus that are just as sophisticated as our standard offerings.
Can you provide full staffing for my event? expand_more
Yes, we offer everything from drop-off service to full-service management including professional servers, bartenders, and event coordinators who are trained in high-end hospitality.
