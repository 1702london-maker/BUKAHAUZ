Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, including your name, email address, phone number, and event details when you make a booking or enquiry. We also collect usage data through analytics to improve our services.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to process bookings, respond to enquiries, send event confirmations, and occasionally share curated updates about Bukahauz experiences. We never sell your personal data to third parties.

Data Sharing

We may share your information with trusted service partners (payment processors, event staff) solely to fulfil your booking. All partners are bound by confidentiality agreements and GDPR-compliant data practices.

Your Rights (GDPR)

You have the right to access, rectify, or erase your personal data at any time. You may also object to processing or request data portability. To exercise these rights, contact us at hello@bukahauz.com.

Data Retention

Booking records are retained for 7 years to comply with UK financial regulations. Marketing preferences are retained until you unsubscribe. All other data is deleted within 12 months of your last interaction.

Cookies

We use essential cookies to operate our website and optional analytics cookies to understand how visitors use our site. See our Cookie Policy for full details.

Bukahauz is registered in England and Wales. For privacy-related queries, contact our Data Controller at hello@bukahauz.com.
