Planning
Intelligently

Transparency is the bedrock of our consultancy. We answer the technicalities so you can focus on the vision.

What is the ideal lead time for planning?

expand_more

For grand events (100+ guests), we recommend 6 to 9 months. For intimate gatherings, 2 to 3 months allows us to secure premium vendors and seasonal ingredients at their peak.

How do you handle dietary requirements?

expand_more

We view dietary restrictions as a creative opportunity rather than a limitation. We design integrated menus where vegan or allergen-free options are as sophisticated as the primary courses.

Do you manage external vendors?

expand_more

Yes. As a full-service consultancy, we act as the central hub, coordinating with your selected florist, entertainment, and rentals to ensure everyone is operating on the same narrative timeline.

Is on-site staff training part of the service?

expand_more

Every Bukahauz event includes a dedicated Floor Lead who conducts an intensive briefing for all staff, covering menu storytelling, plating etiquette, and the evening's specific logistics.
