Frequently Asked Questions

Do you cater to specific dietary requirements? expand_more

Absolutely. Our consultation process includes a deep dive into any allergies, intolerances, or lifestyle choices (Vegan, Keto, Halal, etc.) to ensure every guest enjoys a seamless experience.

What is the minimum and maximum guest count? expand_more

We specialize in intimate gatherings starting from 2 guests. For larger celebrations, our 'Feast' package comfortably accommodates up to 24 guests in a residential setting.

How far do your chefs travel? expand_more

We currently serve the greater metropolitan area and surrounding regions within a 50-mile radius. For international or destination dining, please contact our concierge for a bespoke quote.

Our concierge is ready to help you plan an evening that will be talked about for years. Let us handle the details while you savor the moment.
