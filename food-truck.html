Gourmet Soul, Mobile Heart.

For us, street food isn't just about convenience—it's about the vibrant energy of communal eating. We’re bringing Afro-Caribbean and African heritage to the forefront of the mobile culinary world.

Imagine the smokiness of West African Suya, the comforting spice of Caribbean curries, and the freshness of coastal seafood—all prepared with the precision of a high-end kitchen and served in the heart of your neighborhood.

Curiosities & Details

What’s on the debut menu? expand_more
Our launch menu features our signature Jollof Arancini, Slow-Braised Oxtail Sliders, and a Grilled Corn with Hibiscus-Infused Butter. We will also feature weekly 'Regional Rotations' highlighting specific islands or countries.
How can I track the truck daily? expand_more
Upon launch, we will have a live GPS tracker on our website and real-time updates on our Instagram stories. You can also sign up for SMS alerts for your specific neighborhood.
Do you offer vegan options? expand_more
Absolutely. Over 40% of our menu is plant-based, featuring items like our Roasted Yam Tacos and Callaloo Cream Croquettes. We prioritize inclusivity in every dish.
