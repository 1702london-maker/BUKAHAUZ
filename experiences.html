Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book a Private Dining experience? expand_more
For weekend bookings, we recommend at least 4 weeks in advance. Weekday experiences can often be accommodated with 2 weeks notice depending on seasonal demand.
Can you accommodate specific dietary requirements for corporate events? expand_more
Absolutely. We specialize in inclusive gastronomy. Our chefs will work with you to ensure all guests—regardless of allergies or lifestyle choices—enjoy a cohesive and delicious meal.
Do you provide your own glassware and linens? expand_more
Yes, Bukahauz offers full service. We provide premium linens, glassware, cutlery, and artisanal dinnerware to ensure the aesthetic matches the culinary quality.
